- Real answer: The paper defines two conditional oracles:

* **Quality oracle** – selects the earliest state where the frozen probe already yields the correct answer, regardless of whether the native system would also be correct. Its exact‑match score (EM) serves as an empirical upper bound on answer quality over the frozen states【1†L7-L9】.  

* **Native‑preserving cost oracle** – also stops at the earliest correct probe, but only for questions that the native Search‑R1 system already answers correctly.
